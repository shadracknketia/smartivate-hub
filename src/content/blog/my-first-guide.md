## Why Automation Matters
Before we look at the tools, we need to understand the mindset. Automation isn't about being lazy; it's about **leverage**.
* **Saves Time:** Frees up hours for deep work.
* **Reduces Errors:** Bots don't make typos.
* **Scales Instantly:** What works for 1 client works for 100.
